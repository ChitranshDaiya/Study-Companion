Reality Check

Agar aapka baccha Class 8–12 me hai, toh yeh familiar lagega:

  • ⏰ 3–4 ghante table pe baithta hai — roz, bina miss kiye
  • 📱 Beech beech me phone "check" karta hai — bas ek second ke liye
  • 📖 Kabhi kabhi bas book khuli hoti hai, dimaag kahin aur hota hai

Asli Samasya

Problem yeh nahi hai ki bacche padhna nahi chahte.

Motivation theek hai. Intention bhi hai. Problem yeh hai ki padhne ke time pe padhai hoti hi nahi.

Distraction silent hota hai. Koi shor nahi. Koi fight nahi. Parent ko dikhta bhi nahi.

Isliye: Effort aur result kabhi match nahi karte.

Kaise Kaam Karega

Roz ka study session kaise chalega?

Har din ka study time manually track karne ki zarurat nahi. System simple rakha hai:

🪑
Baccha study table pe baithta hai Device uski presence detect karna shuru karta hai
▶️
Ek button dabaya — session start Koi complex setup nahi. Ek tap, aur system active
⚠️
Beech me uthta hai ya focus break hota hai System detect karta hai → timer automatically pause
✅
Wapas aaya, properly padh raha hai Tabhi — aur sirf tabhi — study time count hota hai
Important Part

Sirf baithne ka time count nahi hota.
Sirf actual padhai ka time count hota hai.
